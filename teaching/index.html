  • Ph.D.
    Bridging Experiments, High-Fidelity Simulations and Advanced Diagnostics in Propulsion Systems Incoming Lecturer, with Prof. S. Carlotti · from 2027

    Experimental, numerical and bridge lectures. My numerical lectures cover high-fidelity CFD of compressible and reactive flows, turbulence–chemistry interaction modeling, multiphase flows, and how to extract engineering quantities from high-fidelity simulations.

    Ph.D. in Aerospace Engineering · More information soon

  • M.Sc.
    Computational Techniques for Thermochemical Propulsion Teaching assistant, course by Prof. F. Piscaglia · since 2020

    Finite-volume methods for turbulent reacting flows, taught with the open-source code OpenFOAM: algorithms for compressible flows, turbulent mixing, combustion modeling of premixed and non-premixed flames, Lagrangian sprays and wall films, Volume of Fluid methods for multiphase flows and mesh motion.

    M.Sc. in Aeronautical Engineering, 2nd year · 8 CFU · in English

    Aerodynamics Teaching assistant, course by Prof. M. Quadrio (Prof. L. Cortelezzi until 2024) · since 2021
