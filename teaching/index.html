Bridging Experiments, High-Fidelity Simulations and Advanced Diagnostics in Propulsion Systems Incoming
Ph.D. course · Lecturer, co-taught with Prof. S. Carlotti

Experimental, numerical and bridge lectures. My numerical lectures cover high-fidelity CFD of compressible and reactive flows, turbulence–chemistry interaction modeling, multiphase flows, and how to extract engineering quantities from high-fidelity simulations. More information soon.

Computational Techniques for Thermochemical Propulsion
M.Sc. in Aeronautical Engineering, 2nd year · Teaching assistant, course held by Prof. F. Piscaglia

Finite-volume methods for turbulent reacting flows, taught with the open-source code OpenFOAM: algorithms for compressible flows, turbulent mixing, combustion modeling of premixed and non-premixed flames, Lagrangian sprays and wall films, Volume of Fluid methods for multiphase flows and mesh motion. 8 CFU, in English.

Aerodynamics
M.Sc. in Aeronautical Engineering, 1st year · Teaching assistant, course held by Prof. L. Cortelezzi (2021-2024) and Prof. M. Quadrio (from 2025)

From potential flow and thin airfoil theory to finite wings and the lifting line, compressible flows and shock waves, panel and vortex-lattice methods and CFD, bluff bodies and high-incidence flows, up to rotor and helicopter aerodynamics. 10 CFU, in English.

Aerospace Propulsion (Propulsione Aerospaziale)
B.Sc. in Aerospace Engineering, 3rd year · Teaching assistant, course held by Prof. C. Paravan
