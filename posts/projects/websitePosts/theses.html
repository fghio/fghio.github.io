Introduction

The theses page lists the M.Sc. theses I offer, with a search box, a filter for the available ones and a counter. All the theses are in one file, thesis-card.html, loaded into the page.

A thesis card

Each thesis is a card with a title, a status, a short description that can be expanded, the expected outputs and the recommended background. Two attributes describe the card for the filters: 

<div class="box card" data-status="open"
     data-tags="hydrogen les detonation openfoam">
  <h2 class="title">Parametric LES of a Hydrogen Rotating Detonation Engine</h2>
  <span class="tag">Open</span>
  ...
</div>

The status is one of open, in-progress, finished or coming (coming soon), each with its own colour.

Search and filters

The search box filters the cards while typing, looking in the title, the description and the tags. The checkbox “Only available theses” hides everything that is not open. The counter at the top shows how many theses are still available. 

var queryMatch  = query === "" || title.includes(query)
                  || description.includes(query) || tags.includes(query);
var statusMatch = !showOpenOnly || status === "open";
$(card).toggle(queryMatch && statusMatch);

The page also accepts the query from the address, so theses?q=hydrogen opens the page already filtered. The site search uses this to open a single thesis.

Before the email

Students who want a thesis are asked to fill in a short form before writing. The page has two buttons: one opens the form, the other (“Apply”) prepares an email. Before the email opens, a small dialog asks whether the form has been filled in: Yes opens the email, No opens the form instead. The dialog is the same component used for the “Hire me” button in the left column.
