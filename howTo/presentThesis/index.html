How to prepare your thesis presentation

This page explains how to prepare the presentation for your M.Sc. thesis defence: timing, slides, structure and what to bring on the day. If you follow these indications, there is no need to rehearse the presentation with me.

Timing

The presentation lasts 15 minutes, followed by 5 minutes of questions. You must stay within the time.

As a rule of thumb, count about one minute per slide: this gives a total of about 15 slides. You can add extra slides, not numbered, after the thank-you slide: they are useful to answer questions.

Template and software

The template is up to you. If you want to start from something ready, you can use the Politecnico di Milano template.

I recommend PowerPoint rather than LaTeX, simply because it is more convenient for a presentation.

Title slide

The first slide contains:

  • the title of the thesis;
  • the date of the defence;
  • your first name and surname;
  • the first name and surname of your supervisor.

Footer and slide numbers

Every slide must be numbered, showing also the total number of slides (e.g., 1/15). I suggest this footer on every slide:

  • bottom left: N. Surname – Title of the presentation;
  • bottom right: slide number / total number of slides.
Results
M. Rossi – High-fidelity simulation of a scramjet combustor 9/15

The extra slides after the thank-you slide are not counted in the total.

Structure

The presentation follows the same logic as your thesis:

  1. Title slide (see above).
  2. Agenda: it is best to use the second slide to show how the presentation is organized.
  3. Introduction: frames the problem.
  4. Governing equations.
  5. Methodology: one slide, or more if it is long.
  6. Test cases: presents the cases studied, indicating the post-processing (where and what you extract to produce the plots).
  7. Validation, if present.
  8. Results, and performance if needed.
  9. Conclusions.
  10. Thank you: the last slide, which separates the presentation from the extra slides.

On the day

To make the procedure easier, come with:

  • your own computer;
  • a USB stick with the presentation in both PowerPoint and PDF format.

There is no need to upload the presentation anywhere: neither on the Politecnico websites nor on any other website.
