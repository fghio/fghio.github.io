How to book a meeting

If you are doing your thesis with me, you do not need to email me for a meeting: book one of the weekly slots yourself. Students of my courses should instead email me to ask for an appointment.

1. One meeting per week

You can book at most one meeting per week. This keeps slots free for everyone. If you book more than one slot in the same week, the extra bookings will be cancelled. For anything urgent, write me an email.

2. Book a slot

  Book a slot (link coming soon)

  1. Open the booking page and pick a free slot.
  2. Write your name and the title of your thesis.
  3. Add one line on what you want to discuss.

3. Before the meeting

  • Push your latest work to your thesis repository, so I can look at it.
  • Prepare the two or three points you want to discuss, with the figures or results you need to show.

4. If you cannot come

Cancel the booking from the confirmation email as soon as you know, so that someone else can use the slot.
